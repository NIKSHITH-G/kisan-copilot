FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Prices: the government Agmarknet daily feed (data.gov.in), full national snapshot pulled twice a day by a Snowflake task. Weather: Open-Meteo, on demand for any Indian district. Agronomy: a curated corpus distilled from public PJTSAU/ICAR package-of-practices material.

How fresh is it?

Prices refresh at 13:30 and 18:30 IST daily. Cards always show the true mandi arrival date, never a vague "updated X ago".

Which languages work?

The demo speaks and understands Telugu, Hindi and English via Sarvam AI; the same pipeline extends to India's 22 scheduled languages (e.g. via Bhashini).

Is this official agricultural advice?

No. Dosages and MSP figures are indicative and source-labelled. Always confirm chemicals and doses with your local agriculture officer.

What if there is no price for my crop here?

It tells you exactly that, shows when your mandi last reported, and quotes the nearest market that has real data — clearly labelled as not local.
